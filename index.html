About

As a graphic designer with 8 months of industry experience, Mohamed Natheer approaches every brief as raw material. By pairing systematic grids with a sharp eye for typographic detail, he builds visual identities that scale effortlessly from a business card to a billboard. Spanning brand identity, packaging, editorial layout, and digital design, the work always starts from the constraint and grows outward.
